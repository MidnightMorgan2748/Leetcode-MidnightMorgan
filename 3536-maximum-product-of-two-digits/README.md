<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-product-of-two-digits">3536. Maximum Product of Two Digits</a></h2><h3>Easy</h3><hr><p>You are given a positive integer <code>n</code>.</p>

<p>Return the <strong>maximum</strong> product of any two digits found in <code>n</code>. You are allowed to use the same digit twice if it appears more than once in the number.</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong class="example">Example 1:</strong></p>
<div class="example-block">
<p><strong>Input:</strong> <span class="example-io">n = 31</span></p>
<p><strong>Output:</strong> <span class="example-io">3</span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong></p>
<p>The digits in <code>n</code> are 3 and 1. The maximum product of two digits is <code>3 * 1 = 3</code>.</p>
</div>

<p><strong class="example">Example 2:</strong></p>
<div class="example-block">
<p><strong>Input:</strong> <span class="example-io">n = 22</span></p>
<p><strong>Output:</strong> <span class="example-io">4</span></p>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong></p>
<p>The digits in <code>n</code> are 2 and 2. The maximum product of two digits is <code>2 * 2 = 4</code>.</p>
</div>

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Constraints:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li><code>10 &lt;= n &lt;= 10<sup>9</sup></code></li>
</ul>
